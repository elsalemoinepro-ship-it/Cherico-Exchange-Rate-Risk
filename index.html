Cherico signs a chicory export contract with a Japanese buyer.

Cherico

French roasted-chicory beverage brand

The French brand Cherico, which produces a roasted-chicory coffee alternative, wants to export a shipment to a Japanese retailer. The Japanese client wants to pay the contract in ¥ (yen), the French would prefer in € (euro) — each side trying to avoid the exchange rate risk. The contract is worth €45,000 and is payable at 60 days, as is common practice in B2B trade.

This time, the Japanese buyer wins the negotiation: the contract will be invoiced in ¥, at a rate of 1€ = ¥162 on the day of signature.
